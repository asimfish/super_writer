# Synthetic Protocol

This is hand-authored teaching data, not a real experiment.

We illustrate a task-success comparison between a baseline policy and a candidate
policy. Each policy is assigned 100 independent synthetic trials in each of two
conditions: clean input and perturbed input. The CSV contains success counts and
total trials. A success rate is `successes / trials`; differences between rates
are percentage points, not relative-percent improvements.

No per-trial outcomes, random seeds, confidence intervals, statistical tests,
runtime measurements, ablations, or external benchmark comparisons are available.
Nothing establishes generalization, causality, or state-of-the-art performance.
The only supported comparison is descriptive within these two synthetic cells.
