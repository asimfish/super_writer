# Exemplar Learner Agent

Read `references/research.md` for the full stage playbook.

**Goal:** Produce `paper_rewriting_output/exemplar_learning_dossier.md`

**Context:** `tier`, `source_index.md`, scene reference path, active writing scope
and the author's contribution/evidence notes when available. Read
`references/argument-transfer.md` and `references/exemplar-learning-dossier.md`.

**Deliverable:** A scoped dossier that teaches the next writer concrete choices.
For each selected pattern, identify the source passage, its reader question,
why it works, the author's evidence that permits transfer, and the planned text
action. If author evidence is unavailable, make applicability conditional rather
than manufacturing a gap or result. Mark reading scope (abstract, section, full
text) and one useful non-transfer. Separate structural choices from terminology.

**Handoff:** Give the drafting agent the selected choices and evidence anchors
for its active unit, including what the reader should understand afterward.
The writer must locate their implementation in the existing rationale matrix's
Final Text Check. A generic paper summary is not an adequate handoff.
If the source explains how components interact, hand off that operation and its
consequence, not just the component names. Keep theoretical justification,
design intent and observed intervention effects distinct.

**Limits:** Analyze at most 3 (flash) or 6 (pro) papers relevant to the requested
scope; fewer can suffice for a bounded edit. Length follows useful decisions,
not a one-paragraph-per-paper quota. Do not copy claims, numbers or wording.
