# Abstract Rhetoric: Source-Calibrated Naturalness

Use this reference when a draft is accurate but sounds like a compressed Methods
section, a list of experiment facts, or a sequence of translated English clauses.
It is an editorial transfer aid, not an official conference template and not a
quality guarantee.

## What to Transfer

Before writing, build a compact source-shaped profile from two or three
contribution-matched abstracts:

| Field | Record |
| --- | --- |
| Opening contract | What problem, capability or resource does the first sentence make salient? |
| Contribution level | Does the abstract name a method, resource, diagnosis, system or theory before implementation details? |
| Move sequence | How do problem, approach, evidence and implication depend on one another? |
| Evidence span | Does the result give a representative number, a benchmark-level finding, a range of tasks, or a qualitative conclusion? |
| Ending function | Does the last sentence establish transfer, a second contribution, public resource, or a bounded implication? |

These observations describe the source's information level and dependencies. They
do not authorize copying a sentence, transplanting a result, or adding the same
number to a new paper. Keep the profile separate from the author's fact rows.

## Natural Abstract Pass

For a conventional empirical AI abstract, test this flexible arc:

1. Make the research problem or useful capability visible.
2. State the contribution at the level a reader needs; name one mechanism only
   when it explains the contribution.
3. Give the evidence at the level of the claim: a benchmark finding, representative
   comparison, or tested scope. Do not narrate every table row.
4. End with the implication, transfer condition, resource, or second contribution
   that makes the paper worth remembering.

This is a reading aid, not a required four-sentence template. A resource paper may
open with the resource; a diagnostic paper may put the counterexample first. The
test is whether the next sentence makes the previous one more intelligible, not
whether all four moves appear.

## Diagnose Artificial Prose

Before adding a transition, ask:

- Does the draft introduce implementation nouns before telling the reader why
  the method exists?
- Could a reader remove one sentence without changing the argument? If so, the
  sequence may be a result inventory rather than a progression.
- Are three or more adjacent sentences at the same information level, such as
  component → component → component or task score → task score → task score?
- Does a mechanism sentence explain a contribution-level consequence, or merely
  restate its modules in longer form?
- Does the ending open a second contribution, or merely append limitations and
  another metric?

Repair the information level first. Then repair sentence rhythm. Vocabulary
lookup and functional sentence cards come after those decisions. Do not force
"therefore", "moreover", or active voice into a passage whose evidence does not
support the implied relation.

## Chinese Delivery

The Chinese version is a scholarly rewrite, not a word-by-word mirror. Read it
alone, rebuild English modifier chains into Chinese clause order, and keep the
technical subject, scope, comparison and uncertainty bound. Avoid adding words
such as “仅”, “因此”, “显著” or “首次” unless the source supports their force.
Check the Chinese against the English after the standalone read, not sentence by
sentence during first drafting.

## Evaluation Boundary

An original abstract is a reference for rhetorical structure, not a controlled
baseline: it may have richer author knowledge, different space and a different
publication context. Source-calibrated demonstrations should preserve raw drafts,
record source access and audit facts separately. Naturalness judgments need fresh,
preferably blind readers on unseen papers; a page comparison or same-model review
does not establish improvement.

The current repository demonstration is in
[`evals/abstract-natural/`](../evals/abstract-natural/). It is intentionally
known-case development and does not promote this guide to a default quality claim.
