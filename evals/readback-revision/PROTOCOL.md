# Prose read-back revision pilot

Recorded before reading test bodies or generating prose on 2026-10-11.
The skill revision is informed by the previous Equivariant Diffusion Policy and
ReMix failures. Those papers are development data, not fresh test cases.

Select VQ-BeT (ICML 2024) and Imitation Bootstrapped Reinforcement Learning
(RSS 2024) from official proceedings PDFs, subject to availability and absence
from this repository's documented development cases. The initial discovery
plan misidentified VQ-BeT/RISE as RSS 2024 papers; it is corrected from official
metadata before reading test bodies or generating prose. The replacement is
venue/source-driven, not based on generated quality outcomes.
Use only Methods, Experiments and explicitly separated limitations. Exclude
author abstracts, Introduction, conclusion summaries, acknowledgments and
references. Record exact extraction boundaries and PDF/input hashes before
generation. Do not repair extracted tables or equations. The coordinator may
see paper metadata during source discovery and body text to define boundaries;
that is not writer access. Proceedings metadata pages include author abstracts
visible to the coordinator; these are not used to write the already-frozen
candidate guide or supplied to writers. Model pretraining exposure cannot be
excluded.

Two fresh initial-writer contexts each receive the same two bodies and the
shared abstract/section guides, generating one abstract (150-200 words) and
Results paragraph (120-180 words) per paper, with at most 120 words of notes.
Keep both initial generations unchanged; do not seed errors or select a weak
draft. Each corresponding pair of editors receives the exact same initial
generation and source, and has one revision response, with at most 120 words of
internal revision notes per case. Four fresh editors: two samples in each
condition. These are equal-pass revisions, not candidate revision versus an
unrevised baseline, and not the original writer revising its own hidden thoughts.

Baseline editing guidance: shared abstract/section guides and the argument
guide as it stood immediately before this turn. Candidate: identical guidance,
with only the new prose-only read-back section added to the argument guide.
The candidate guide is frozen before reading the test bodies. Matrix and
build/rewrite routing edits are not included in this isolated guide comparison.
Both conditions may use notes and evidence. Neither may browse, read publisher
abstracts, other outputs, prior development reports or additional skill files.

Save all six writer/editor responses untouched. No post-generation editing,
padding, response selection or retries. Retain word-budget violations and
failed tasks. The repository word-token rule checks length, not writing quality.

Two fresh reviewers read the same raw bodies and eight anonymous revision pairs
(two papers, two initial drafts, two units); their A/B order is reversed. No
condition, guide, initial draft, revision notes, prior result or expected
preference is exposed. Review source fidelity, contribution, argument,
selection and language; allow ties and require a located reason. A separate
fresh source auditor checks all revised texts against designated PDF body
pages/tables, with no condition labels or quality judgments. Unblind after both
quality reviews are saved. Source concerns take precedence over stylistic wins.

Report each sample and disagreement, not just judgment totals. The two reviewer
votes are not independent experiments; editors handle both papers, and repeated
samples share source context. This is a small same-family model revision pilot,
not a human study, full-workflow test, guaranteed unseen-paper evaluation,
training run, broad superiority claim or acceptance prediction. Published author
abstracts are not a scored condition. Test papers become development data after
this round. Third-party PDFs/body text stay local; public artifacts can include
generated prose, frozen guides, exact receipts, prompts with portable paths,
anonymous pairs stripped of source text and raw judgments.
