## Equal requested budgets and bounded contexts

Three fixed groups contain two distinct-function papers each. Two fresh initial
contexts per group produce one full-paper abstract 150-200 words and working
notes <=100 words for each paper: 6 initial contexts / 12 initial abstracts.
Each exact initial/source/notes packet goes to a fresh baseline and candidate
editor, ONE revision each under the same requested budgets: 12 editor contexts,
24 final abstracts, 12 paired cases. No extra pass, padding, quality retries,
raw response repair, winner selection or silent sample exclusion. Record every
budget/schema exception. Same-group model effects and two replicates per paper
are clustered, not independent paper observations.

## Blind preference, independent reading and source audits

For each group two anonymous reviewer contexts assess four pairs with exactly
reversed A/B labels. They read only assigned selected bodies and final prose,
not author abstracts, working notes, frozen critical questions, guides, generation
prompts, mappings or previous judgments. Fidelity pass/concern/fail is separate
from 1-5 argument/language scores and A/B/Tie preference. Reason <=100 words.
Ties are allowed. No mandatory imitation of source wording or maximum number count.

Two fresh source-free reader contexts per group each assess four single texts:
balanced conditions, never both counterparts of one paper/sample. They may see
another replicate of the paper; disclose that limit. Generic questions recover
the contribution/operative relationship and consequential comparison/condition.
Use prose only; do not fill gaps from scientific recall. Core and scope witnesses
are literal substrings <=18 words or empty; all response fields total <=130 words.

After readers finish, two further source-grounded auditors per group assess all
eight anonymous texts/readings against selected body ONLY. They receive the
pre-frozen critical question but no gold expected answer, labels or preferences.
Classify actual support as supported/missing/contradicted/uncertain and reader
support as text_grounded/inferred/wrong/unanswered/ambiguous, separately from
fidelity. Reason <=100 words. Successful reader rejection of a contradiction does
not repair prose truth; a source-correct guess does not repair missing text.
Six review + six reader + six auditor contexts give 24 preferences, 24 readings
and 48 audit judgments. Finish ALL planned responses before decoding. Preserve
all independent labels, disagreement and uncertainty, with no judge replacement.

All agents inherit the same model family, fork_context=false, no model override,
no external SDK or paid-API runner. Prompt restrictions are not filesystem
quarantine or absence of model recall. Main implements/checks orchestration and
archive while source/behavior contexts own disjoint outputs; max six open agents.

## Prospective next-stage decision, not a statistical quality threshold

Consider the unchanged candidate eligible for another abstract-focused/human
validation only if candidate preferences exceed baseline in BOTH sample strata
and at least FOUR of SIX paper strata; at least two pairs unanimously favor it;
no pair unanimously favors baseline; candidate has no fidelity fail or material
new concern where its baseline passes; and all candidate abstracts meet budget.
Incomplete/schema-failed assessment prevents eligibility. All exceptions remain
in all-case reporting rather than being discarded. Budget violations in baseline
are disclosed, not used to claim candidate correctness. Failing the criterion
does not justify lowering it or retesting these cases as fresh data.

This is a deliberately conservative operational continuation criterion, NOT
population significance, human understanding, accepted-paper imitation,
citation entailment or evidence of full-skill superiority. Even if it passes,
do not automatically promote runtime guidance or pool earlier votes. Report all
paper/sample results, arguments, fidelity/support, word budgets and disagreements.
Papers become development material after this round. Source body/PDF/full metadata/
extraction recipes remain local; public archive retains original generated prose,
judgments, anonymous generated-text packets, guides and structured source receipts.
