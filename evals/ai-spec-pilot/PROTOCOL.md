# Current AI Specification: Prepared Pilot Design

Status: **prepared, not executed**. No drafts, readers, preference votes or
quality outcomes exist for this pilot. Independent agent authorization and a
checked, version-consistent body/fact packet remain prerequisites. Freeze an
execution record before generation; do not treat this design as completed evidence.

## Question and Conditions

Does selected guidance from the current 0.4 AI specification improve recoverable
argument relationships and calibrated terminology without reducing contribution
retention or prose cohesion? A clearer term alone is not an overall writing gain.

Both conditions use identical author-evidence packets, unit instructions, common
guides, budgets and edit passes. Common guides are `references/argument-transfer.md`,
`references/abstract-decisions.md` and `references/writing-protocols.md` from
`bfc1406e7c3778d4154930d6d158049e84b3736d`. Its argument-transfer guide is the
unchanged default, not the previously rejected operative-relation candidate.

- Baseline: common guides and normal evidence-linked planning/read-back.
- Candidate: the same common guides, plus at most four relevant complete AI-spec
  records per unit. Preselect IDs before drafting and retain conditions,
  exceptions and source context. Use the clarity-context guide only for an actual
  referent/modifier or procedure problem; do not manufacture one.

Record hashes for guides, exact retrieved records, materials and prompt texts.
The candidate is an incremental context treatment, not a new model, more editing
passes or proof of having learned an author's style. Guide exposure is necessarily
different; token budgets and actual returned lengths must be reported.

## Generation and Assessment

Planned pilot: two papers, two replicates, three units (Abstract, Methods, Results),
yielding 12 pairs and 24 final passages. Use fresh inherited-model contexts with
no parent conversation fork or model override. Keep condition exposures separate.
No generation is run until independent-agent authorization is supplied.

For each unit, allow one initial draft and one edit under identical passes in
both conditions; retain every original response. Proposed final budgets are
150-190 words for Abstract, 110-150 for Methods, 120-160 for Results, with separate
brief editorial notes of at most 60 words. Finalize the counting rule before
generation. Violations are outcomes, not grounds to pad, silently revise or rerun.

After writers finish, create randomized A/B packets with a saved mapping hidden
from assessors. A second preference reviewer receives reversed presentation
order. Neither gets condition names, prompts, editorial notes, prior outcomes
or author abstracts. Permit ties and ask for a located decisive reason.

Separate assessment roles:

1. Preference reviewers: contribution retention, argument clarity, evidence
   selection, technical calibration and natural scholarly language; use the
   same declared evidence summary, and distinguish source uncertainty.
2. Prose-only readers: answer frozen operation/contrast/scope questions from
   final text, citing exact witnesses and allowing unrecoverable answers. Do
   not supply reference answers or source facts to these readers.
3. Source-grounded auditor: check claims and reader witnesses against the
   selected source packet; distinguish missing text, contradicted text,
   reader-added specificity and source uncertainty.

An inherited-model fresh context is not an independent human or different-model
judgment. Prompt-only read restrictions are not an OS access boundary. Use narrow
authorized input files, record actual exposures and avoid claims of perfect blinding.
Do not ask a reviewer to judge its own generated draft.

## Decision Rule and Integrity

Report votes by unit/paper/replicate, unanimous outcomes, disagreements,
consequential source failures, recovery/witness support and budget violations
separately. Do not pool older rounds or treat the 24 correlated reviewer votes
as 24 independent experiments. Ordinal-score means are descriptive only.

This small pilot can justify further validation only if candidate abstract and
methods preferences both net favor the candidate, at least two pairs receive
unanimous candidate preference across distinct papers, no candidate consequential
fidelity failure appears, and both conditions satisfy the same final budgets.
Failure leaves the result negative or inconclusive; do not relax criteria after
unblinding. Passing is not runtime promotion, superiority to published abstracts,
human validity, conference acceptance or general writing improvement.

No posterior source substitution, reviewer replacement, raw-response correction,
or silent exclusion is allowed. Infrastructure failures are logged separately;
any replacement attempt and its reason stay visible. A new registry/material
choice requires a new pre-generation revision and exposure record.
