# Exploratory comparison with author abstracts

Added after all thirty-six revisions were saved, while the primary anonymous
reviews were still running and before primary preference outcomes were observed.
This does not alter the frozen primary pilot or its gate.

Extract each original abstract from the same RSS PDF's first page and compare it
with the already-saved compression-first abstract. Original text stays in a
private local packet and is not published or shown to any primary writer. Publish
source URLs, original-text hashes and word counts, label mappings, exact dispatch
messages, and returned judgments. Reviewer reasons paraphrase the author text
instead of reproducing its sentences.

Two new reviewers each judge all six anonymous pairs; the second gets opposite
labels and reversed paper order. They compare scientific focus, organization,
economy, natural disciplinary language and overall quality. No style is declared
better merely for being shorter or more complete. A reader may prefer the author
version for a clearer problem, more distinctive insight or a more coherent arc.

This is an absolute-quality diagnostic, not a fair treatment-effect test. The
author wrote from the full paper, while our writers received selected body facts
and a short initial draft. Lengths, information scope and writing conditions
differ. No causal fidelity verdict is made from the abstracts alone. The
comparison cannot repair or override a failed primary gate.
