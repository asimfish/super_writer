# Proposed operative-relation revision follow-up

Prospective design only. Enter this experiment only if every prespecified
calibration admission criterion passes. No paper body is read, draft generated
or writing gain claimed by creating this file.

## Narrow intervention

Baseline is the current argument-transfer guide, including its read-back check.
Candidate adds only the independently frozen `candidate_hypothesis.md` section:
protect the operative relation and indispensable condition in the actual claim
while compressing, rather than retaining labels or replacing explanation with
assumption lists. Shared abstract, measured-event preflight and section guidance
are identical. The preceding twelve papers and the synthetic controls are
development material, not new held-out evaluation cases.

## Intended fresh-paper coverage

Two purposively selected papers in distinct functions: an empirical robotic
learning/system method and a theoretical guarantee under distribution change.
First candidates are Learning to Walk in Minutes Using Massively Parallel Deep
Reinforcement Learning and Conformal Prediction Beyond Exchangeability. Verify
actual titles, official publication records, available complete source versions
and documented repository exposure AFTER admission. Do not infer absence from
model pretraining. If source unavailable, record exclusion and replace before
any generation, never based on observed prose quality.

Freeze exact source inputs, version/PDF/body SHA-256 and unit-specific critical
questions before generation. Include complete relevant main-body Methods,
Analysis/Proof and Experiments plus located definitions/limitations needed for
the claimed relation; do not silently omit essential theorem definitions because
they occur in a different section. Exclude author abstract and source summary
as writer targets. Preserve original mixed findings and raw extraction defects.
Source PDFs, body publisher prose and source-containing packets remain local.

Two fresh initial samples per paper, each producing Abstract (150-200 words),
Methods/Analysis (110-160), Results/Example interpretation (120-180) and notes
at most 120 words. Each exact initial/source/notes packet goes to fresh baseline
and candidate editors, one revision each at equal budgets. This yields twelve
pairs and twenty-four final passages, not a broad general-effectiveness sample.

Two reversed-order anonymous preference contexts per paper give twelve judgments
per paper. Two source-free reader contexts per paper cover both samples and
all units, balanced versions, never both counterparts of the same unit/sample.
They may see other replicates of the paper, a disclosed contamination limit.
Separate source-grounded fine auditors answer pre-frozen critical questions and
inspect reconstruction support, without condition names or preferences. Critical
questions identify necessary function, not a mandatory inventory or gold wording.

No raw editing, quality selection, padding, response repair or extra candidate
revision. Finish every planned assessment before decoding. Record all missing
responses, schema/budget failures, disputed facts and preferences. Model family
is inherited without override or forked history. A pass in synthetic controls
is not validated human comprehension, complete entailment or permission to
treat a style preference as correct science.

Report all pairs and per-paper/unit/sample differences. Descriptive counts only;
no significance/population effect or acceptance prediction from two papers.
Source-concerned stylistic wins are not unqualified gains. Promote no runtime
guide or advertise improvement solely on the basis of stronger calibration.
If the calibration gate fails, preserve this as an unexecuted proposal and stop
fresh-paper quality testing for this round rather than relaxing the threshold.
