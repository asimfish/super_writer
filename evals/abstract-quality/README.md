# Abstract Quality Calibration

These original, synthetic micro-abstracts test evaluator discrimination. They
are not scientific results, accepted abstracts, generation trials, or evidence
that a skill version improves writing. They deliberately have no venue-length
requirement. Public cases are calibration data, never a held-out benchmark.

Give an evaluator only each case's `materials`, `request`, and `drafts`.
Withhold `oracle` and these instructions about intended contrasts. Randomize
draft labels/order and preserve the permutation. Ask for source-fidelity
findings, contribution retention, and a reasoned preference or tie. Do not
instruct the evaluator to choose the longer draft or reward keyword presence.

The oracle records editorial expectations, not objective quality ground truth.
A disagreement requires examining the reasoning, not repeatedly rerunning until
the evaluator agrees. A real test must record model/context configuration,
actual prompts, untouched responses, and all disagreements. No model evaluation
of these cases is claimed by the schema tests.

Cases cover a factually valid contribution omission, an invalid transfer of a
theoretical guarantee to an approximate implementation, and two defensible
information allocations. Additional cases cover co-contribution loss under
compression, reporting queries separated from manuscript prose, and embodied
transfer/trial-unit errors. The collection also distinguishes supplied
uncertainty from generic disclaimers and benchmark counts from ablation counts.
Three additional development cases distinguish mixed baseline training regimes,
retained design rationale versus a repeated architecture inventory, and equally
defensible method-first versus gap-first openings. They are authored calibration
expectations, not new evidence of generated-writing improvement.
The experimental-paragraph cases exercise adjacent
decisions rather than an abstract-only writing template.
Include both plausible preference and tie cases so
the evaluator cannot succeed by always demanding a change.

## Author-Priority Forward Probes

`priority-probes.json` is a separate three-case synthetic generation probe, not
three additional A/B calibration examples. The first two cases share exactly
the same facts but change the author's priorities. The third removes the reuse
evidence while retaining a desired, unconfirmed claim. Give writers only the
selected facts and author decision, never the oracle. Compare retained meaning
and factual boundaries, not whether every fact or a fixed keyword appears.
The integrity test verifies the counterfactual input relationship, not prose
quality. These public probes become development data after use.
