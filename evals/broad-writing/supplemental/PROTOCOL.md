# Operationally triggered text-only audit replication

Registered after all primary responses were saved, before the coordinator read
or decoded their judgments. This is supplementary, not part of the original
preregistered comparison. Original generations, preferences, reader responses,
anchors and primary audits remain unchanged and are reported separately.

Primary auditors D2, D4 and D6 reported accidental visibility of excluded PDF
summary, Related Work or introductory material during filtering. Their assertion
that they did not use it cannot be verified. Replicate all six domains, including
the three without reported exposure, rather than choosing preferred cases.

Six fresh same-family, non-forked contexts receive the same anonymous 36-text
packets, reader responses, exact locked body text and frozen anchors. They do not
receive PDFs, full raw extractions, metadata, guides, primary audit outputs,
preferences, condition mappings or coordinator history. This withholds additional
source text in the provided inputs; it does not erase model recall or enforce
filesystem isolation. Exactly one response per domain, no generation/revision
retry, raw response repair or post-hoc replacement of primary labels.

Use the same fidelity pass/concern/fail and core/scope recovery 0-2 definitions.
Do not fill ambiguous flattened table/equation evidence from remembered or
excluded source content. Locate uncertainty instead of manufacturing a false
numerical mismatch. Judge the selected paragraph function; anchors are not a
mandatory fact inventory. Outputs go to this directory, outside primary raw/.

Report all 216 supplementary judgments, label/score agreement with the original
216 audits, condition/domain/unit summaries and original reasons. Differences
can reflect assessor variation and reduced layout access, not only accidental
exposure. Do not pool the audits as independent evidence or select the favorable
one. Preference outcomes stay the originally planned 216 votes. These checks
cannot establish human independence, complete entailment or writing superiority.
