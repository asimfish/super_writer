Preserve evidence-bearing relationships ai.rule.evidence / invariant

Part of speech: not applicable. Local editorial status, not global word approval.

Topics: claim_strength, evaluation_protocol. Levels: sentence, paragraph.

Meaning. An edit preserves the relation between a quantity, its entity, measurement unit and condition, not merely the presence of each token.

Action. Check source-to-draft omissions and draft-to-source additions within the requested unit.

Avoid. Keeping two scores but swapping the methods, denominator, metric direction or evaluation setting.

Applies when

  • Apply to factual rewriting, translation and result reporting.

Evidence needed

  • Located author facts and their original relationships.

Exceptions

  • An abstract may omit secondary detail when its remaining claim stays accurate; necessary scope cannot be omitted.

Part of speech: not applicable. Local editorial status, not global word approval.

Topics: citation_support, argument_structure. Levels: sentence, paragraph, document.

Meaning. A reference can teach an explanatory choice without establishing our method's result or novelty.

Action. Transfer the reader-facing function only when the author's materials support it; establish citation support independently.

Avoid. Copying a source paper's mechanism, speedup or claim strength into our paper.

Applies when

  • Use whenever drafting from an exemplar or sentence scaffold.

Evidence needed

  • Exemplar location and a separate author evidence anchor.

Exceptions

  • The same publication may also support a cited claim, but that support must be checked separately.

Part of speech: not applicable. Local editorial status, not global word approval.

Meaning. Conditions that change a result's interpretation remain recoverable where the result is claimed.

Define objects and symbols before using their relationships ai.rule.definition / editorial

Part of speech: not applicable. Local editorial status, not global word approval.

Topics: argument_structure. Levels: word, sentence, paragraph.

Meaning. Definitions make the subject, operation and mathematical dependence recoverable.

Action. Introduce the relevant object and symbol, then explain the relation it participates in. Resolve ambiguous antecedents and modifier attachments using the author's defined relationships, not a guessed actor or forced synonym.

Avoid. Acronym chains, ambiguous pronouns and equations whose quantities are never identified.

Applies when

  • Use for new symbols, resources, modules or ambiguous technical nouns.
  • Use when a pronoun or compact compound leaves two materially different technical readings.

Evidence needed

  • Author definitions and the actual equation or implementation.

Exceptions

  • Standard notation may need only a compact definition; established definitions need not be repeated.
  • Keep unambiguous pronouns and standard compound terms; passive voice can retain the intended information focus when the known actor is not the topic.

Keep consequential uncertainty and its definition ai.rule.uncertainty / invariant

Part of speech: not applicable. Local editorial status, not global word approval.

Topics: statistics, claim_strength. Levels: sentence, paragraph.

Meaning. Dispersion and interval estimates qualify a result; different uncertainty summaries are not interchangeable.

Action. Retain supplied uncertainty where it changes interpretation and state whether it is SD, SE, CI or another declared summary.
